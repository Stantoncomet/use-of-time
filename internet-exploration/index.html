Welcome

This website is built on the idea that, like how pictures can convey more than words can, experiences can help one understand certain philosophies and ideas described in scholarly articles. It consists of two main platforms in which users are encouraged to explore with the idea of freedom of expression in mind. The forum is a chatroom-like experience in which you can converse in a few different conversations with strangers and learn from other’s perspectives. The social media page is less interpersonal, with a flow of content designed to spark questions of identity and conformity by exposing the users to other lives and ideas, with hints of consumerism and how society is told to see things.

Abstract

Individual Exploration via the Internet in Pursuit of Dismantling Heterosexuality

Is the internet a strong enough platform for feminist phenomenology to occur in ways that can disrupt the norms of heterosexual society? This work argues that the freedom offered by the internet creates an environment for people to individually explore and collaborate together on progressive ideas. This isn’t a comparison between the effectivity of real life and online activist movements, but more an exploration of how certain critical networking and social opportunities are more easily found and experienced online.

Starting with the ideas of gender as a performance laid out by Judith Butler, we can explore how individuals have capacity to express themselves authentically. This freedom is more easily expressed when not under the scrutiny of heterosexual society. This argument is furthered by Lorde’s urging us to escape from the “master’s house,” not with the “master’s tools,” but those of our own, created from personal uniqueness. This would come after individuals are comfortable with their differences and understand how that makes them who they are.

The core of this project is an interactive website including a chat room, forum, and social media site. A social media-based website was chosen for its immediate relation to the platforms at the forefront of my thesis, where people can interact with others in ways possibly deemed abnormal for their lives. Users explore the site and engage in fabricated social interactions (a range of pre-written responses and posts, based on real experiences), which are curated as opportunities of self-discovery. To maintain feasibility, these experiences will be centered around a few main topics, including gender expression, finding community, and online activist movements, where users have multiple choices for action.

The freedom of people to express themselves beyond a binary doesn’t only benefit the individual, but also, given platforms to speak and collaborate, can lead to real change to oppressing systems. Exploring the steps in which this occurs shows people can find their way into the feminist movement from a variety of origins.

Keywords: gender performance, individual differences, collaboration, personal experience, web app
